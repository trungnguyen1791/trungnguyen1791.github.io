# SmileBird — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-10
**Last updated:** 2026-10-10

SmileBird ("the app") is built and operated by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your data. It applies to the SmileBird app distributed through the Apple App Store.

## Summary

- **We collect no data.** Nothing about you or your play leaves your device.
- **The camera never records.** Your face is analysed live, on your device, and immediately discarded.
- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.**

## The camera

SmileBird asks for camera access so you can flap by smiling or winking. When you allow it:

- The front camera runs Apple's on-device face tracking (ARKit).
- The app reads only a few numbers per frame: how much you are smiling and how closed each eye is.
- Camera images are never saved, displayed, uploaded or shared. Face data is never stored, except as described below.
- You can deny or revoke camera access any time in **Settings → SmileBird → Camera**. The game then works with taps.

## Data stored on your device

The app keeps these on your device only, in its own storage:

- Your best score.
- Your sound setting.
- Your face calibration: five numbers describing your resting and maximum smile and eye-closing levels, used to tune the controls to your face. It contains no image and cannot identify you.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

## Apple's "App Privacy" label

The App Store listing declares **Data Not Collected**.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. App Store downloads are handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
