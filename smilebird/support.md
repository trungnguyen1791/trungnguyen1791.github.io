# SmileBird — Support

SmileBird is a pixel-art arcade game where you fly by smiling or winking. This page is for help, bug reports, and feature requests.

## Contact

- **Email:** trungnguyen.1791@gmail.com
- **Response time:** usually within 2–3 business days (solo developer, Asia/Saigon timezone).

When emailing about a bug, please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I play?
Smile or wink at your device to make the bird flap, and fly through the gaps between the pillars. Each smile flaps once: relax your face, then smile again to flap again. You can also tap the screen or press space.

### Smiling doesn't work. What can I check?
- Face control needs an iPhone or iPad with face tracking (iPhone XS / XR or newer, iPad 8th generation or newer, iPad Air 3rd generation or newer, iPad mini 5th generation or newer, or any Face ID iPad Pro).
- Allow camera access in **Settings → SmileBird → Camera**.
- Keep your face visible and well lit, about an arm's length from the screen.
- Recalibrate in the app: tap the gear, then **Recalibrate smile & wink**.

### My normal blinks make the bird flap.
They shouldn't: a wink only counts when one eye stays closed while the other stays open. If it still happens, recalibrate from the gear menu.

### I can't wink.
No problem. Smiling alone is enough, and tapping always works.

### Does the app record or upload my face?
No. The camera is analysed live on your device and nothing is saved or sent. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy).

### Does it work on Mac?
On Apple Silicon Macs the iPad version runs with click or space-bar controls. Face control is not available on Mac.

### Is it free?
Yes. The game is supported by ads: a banner on the start and game-over screens and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. There are no ads during play and no in-app purchases.
