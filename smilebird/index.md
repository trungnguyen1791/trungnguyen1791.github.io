# SmileBird: Smile to Fly

**Your face is the controller.**

SmileBird is a pixel-art arcade game for iPhone and iPad. Guide a little bird through a sunset sky full of pillars, and flap by smiling or winking at your device. Tapping works too.

- Smile or wink to flap, using the front camera's face tracking.
- A quick calibration tunes it to your face.
- Live smile and wink meters show what the camera sees.
- Tap, click or press space whenever you prefer.

**No ads. No tracking. No data collected. The camera never records.**
