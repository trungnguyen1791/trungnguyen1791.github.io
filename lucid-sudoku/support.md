# Lucid Sudoku — Support

Lucid Sudoku is a visual sudoku tutor that animates the reasoning behind every move. This page is for help, bug reports, and feature requests.

## Contact

- **Email:** trungnguyen.1791@gmail.com
- **Response time:** usually within 2–3 business days (solo developer, Asia/Saigon timezone).

When emailing about a bug, please include:
- iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
- iPhone / iPad model
- Lucid Sudoku version (Settings → About inside the app)
- A short description of what you did and what happened.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Lucid Sudoku free?
Yes, with a free trial that includes 3 techniques and 5 puzzles. Full access is a one-time $4.99 unlock. No subscription. No ads.

### Does the app work offline?
Yes. Everything runs on-device. No account, no login, no network required to play.

### What data does the app collect?
Anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage analytics (which techniques you complete, bucketed durations, OS-major bucket). Nothing is linked to your identity. No puzzle content is sent off-device. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy) for full detail.

### How do I turn off analytics?
**Settings → Privacy** inside the app. One toggle disables Analytics and Crashlytics together.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the app, go to **Settings → Restore Purchase**. Sign in to the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

### Why does the app refuse to give me the answer?
By design. Lucid Sudoku is a tutor, not a solver. It shows you the *reasoning* that leads to the next move — naked singles, hidden singles, pointing pairs, X-Wings, and more — so you learn to see the puzzle yourself. If you want a one-tap solver, this is not the app for you, and we won't be offended if you say so.

### Which techniques are taught?
Eight, in roughly increasing difficulty: Naked Single, Hidden Single, Naked Pair, Hidden Pair, Pointing Pair, Box/Line Reduction, X-Wing, and a guided "candidate elimination" walkthrough.

### Where do the daily puzzles come from?
Hand-picked by the developer. A new one each day. You can also enter any puzzle manually.

### Does it support VoiceOver / Dynamic Type / Reduce Motion?
Yes, all three. Accessibility is a first-class concern, not an afterthought. If you find an accessibility bug, please report it — those go to the top of the queue.

### Will there be an iPad / Mac version?
The app runs on iPad today. A native Mac version is being considered. No timeline.

### Will there be more techniques later?
Yes. Swordfish, Y-Wing, and Coloring are on the roadmap.
